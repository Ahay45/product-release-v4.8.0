Stream totals, last 30 days — migration 942, 10.7% bounced  ·  chatdaddy-product-release-upda 505, 2.0% bounced  ·  Default Transactional 792, 6.9% bounced  ·  Default Broadcast 696, 26.0% bounced — but nothing new went down it this cycle; its last send was 15 July. Spam complaints are 0% on every stream. Two streams are new: both of this week's campaigns got their own.

Read from Postmark, server 17743391, Eastern Time, on 30 Jul · open and click figures are cumulative to the close of the 30-day window (29 Jul) · opens on the migration send were still arriving on 30 Jul, so 19.2% is a floor, not a final number · transactional totals cover 27–30 Jul only: 19 sent, 16 opened, 0 bounced

Both fixes from last week are done Last week this slide said two things needed fixing: nothing was measurable, and a bulk announcement had gone out on the outbound transactional stream. Both are closed. Open and link tracking are on for both sends, so for the first time these are real numbers rather than blanks — and each campaign now has its own broadcast stream instead of sharing a default, which is where the unsubscribe handling and the reputation live. The default broadcast stream's 26% bounce rate has not been re-run since 15 July; the new streams landed at 2.0% and 10.7%.

What to fix next Two things now stand out. The migration list needs cleaning — 71 hard bounces out of 942 is a legacy segment nobody has pruned, and hard bounces are what damage sending reputation. And click rates are the real gap: 25.3% of people opened the What's New digest but 0.4% of its 3,535 tracked links were clicked. The email is being read and then closed. That is a call-to-action problem, and it is the same pattern the in-app announcements showed before the discovery work — delivery is fixed, engagement is not.
