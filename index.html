The transactional figure mixes the release blast in with daily analytics reports and P0 alerts; the broadcast figure is the release blast plus the internal test.

Read from Postmark, server 17743391, Eastern Time · the 16 Jul broadcast and 21 Jul transactional sends · the only opens on record are the 14-address internal test of the same template — 14 delivered, 12 opened (85.7%) — the one send that had open tracking on

Two things to fix Open and click rates are blank because open tracking is off and no link tracking is configured — not because nobody read them. Until that is switched on, we are sending announcements we cannot measure. The 20 Jul send also went out on the outbound transactional stream rather than broadcast, which is where a bulk announcement belongs and where the managed unsubscribe lives.
