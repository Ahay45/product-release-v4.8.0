THE SAME RULESET AS A PRE-SUBMIT CHECKLIST

  • Category must be Authentication.
  • No URLs, in the content or any parameter.
  • No media header — plain text only.
  • No emojis, including in variable examples.
  • Parameters capped at 15 characters.
  • One-tap autofill is Android-only; iPhone gets copy-code.
  • Zero-tap is Android-only and needs both fallbacks.
  • The Template Library is the fastest route to approval.
